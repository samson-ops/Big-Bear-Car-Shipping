Vehicle shipping for San Jose and the South Bay

San Jose moves cars for different reasons than most cities. A large share of our South Bay shipments are tied to jobs: an engineer relocating to a company's Austin or Seattle campus, a new hire bringing a car from Michigan, a family heading back to Chicago after a few years in Sunnyvale. Another large share involves electric vehicles, which are more common on the roads here than anywhere else in the country and which have a few shipping quirks worth knowing.

Big Bear handles both. Use the quote tool above for an instant, all-in price, or call the San Jose desk at (669) 338-2449 if you want to talk through a relocation timeline or a vehicle with special requirements.

Shipping an electric vehicle from San Jose

Teslas, Rivians, Lucids, Polestars and the EV versions of mainstream brands ship on the same open and enclosed carriers as gasoline cars, with a few differences in how the load is handled:

  • State of charge. Ship with the battery between roughly 30 and 60 percent. It needs enough charge to drive on and off the trailer at both ends, with a margin for a week in transit, but a full battery adds weight and gains nothing.
  • Weight. A Model S or an R1T weighs 1,000 to 2,500 pounds more than a comparable gas vehicle. Carriers are limited by axle weight, so heavy EVs are sometimes priced as a larger vehicle. The quote tool accounts for this when you select the model.
  • Transport mode. Put the car in its transport or tow mode before loading so the driver can roll it and so the parking brake does not engage automatically. Leave a key card or fob with the driver; a phone key alone is not enough.
  • Low ground clearance. Performance trims and air-suspension cars set to their lowest height can scrape on standard ramps. Raise the suspension before pickup, or choose an enclosed carrier with a liftgate.

Where we pick up in Santa Clara County and beyond

Door-to-door service covers San Jose and every city between Gilroy and the San Mateo County line. Most Valley neighborhoods are easy for a full-size hauler: wide suburban streets in Almaden, Evergreen, Cupertino and Sunnyvale allow pickup right in front of the house. The exceptions are the hills. Los Gatos above the Village, Saratoga's canyon roads and the Los Altos Hills estates are better handled at a meeting point on a main road, and your coordinator will set that up ahead of time. Corporate campuses in North San Jose, Mountain View and Palo Alto generally allow pickup in a visitor or loading area if security is notified in advance.

Corporate relocations and new-hire vehicles

If your employer is covering the move, we provide the documentation relocation companies and HR teams ask for: an itemized quote, a written pickup window, the carrier's insurance certificate on request, and a bill of lading with condition reports at origin and destination. We can also schedule pickups through an assistant or office manager and release the vehicle to a named colleague at the destination. For employees arriving in San Jose, inbound shipping works the same way in reverse, with delivery to a home, an extended-stay hotel or a company lot.

What a San Jose shipment costs

Silicon Valley rates track slightly above Los Angeles on the long eastbound lanes because fewer trucks originate here and most drivers add a positioning leg through the Central Valley. On Pacific Northwest and Southern California lanes the gap disappears, since the South Bay sits right on the 101 and the 5 corridor. Beyond distance, your price is driven by vehicle size and weight (which is where heavy EVs come in), open versus enclosed, how soon you need the pickup, and the time of year. Late spring and summer are busiest. If your dates are flexible, a two-week window instead of a fixed day is the single easiest way to lower the number.

Open or enclosed?

Open carriers are the standard and the economical choice for commuters and family cars. Enclosed shipping makes sense for a surprising number of South Bay vehicles: performance EVs with expensive paint protection film, collector cars that show at the Palo Alto Concours or the Los Gatos events, and anything with carbon-fiber aero that would not survive a ramp. Enclosed costs about half again as much, and in exchange you get a liftgate, soft straps and a trailer that keeps the car out of the weather and away from road debris across the Sierra and the plains.

Getting ready for pickup

Clean the car so the inspection is accurate, keep fuel to a quarter tank or charge to around half, remove the FasTrak tag and anything loose, and take your own photos. Disable any aftermarket alarm. If you have a car cover, send it along only for enclosed shipments; covers flap and scuff paint on an open trailer. Have the key ready, confirm the meeting point if you live in the hills, and let your coordinator know who will be there if it isn't you. Updates follow at pickup, in transit and on delivery.

San Jose FAQ

San Jose car shipping FAQ

Can I ship a Tesla or other EV from San Jose?
Yes. EVs ship on the same open and enclosed carriers as other cars. Charge to 30–60 percent, put the vehicle in transport mode, raise air suspension if equipped, and leave a physical key card or fob with the driver. Heavy EVs may price as a larger vehicle because of carrier weight limits.
How much does it cost to ship a car from San Jose to Austin or Seattle?
San Jose to Seattle usually runs $700–$950 on an open carrier with two to three days in transit. San Jose to Austin typically falls between $1,000 and $1,350 with four to six days in transit. Enclosed service, large vehicles and expedited pickup add to the price.
Can you pick up at my office in Mountain View or Palo Alto?
In most cases, yes. Corporate campuses usually allow pickup in a visitor lot or loading area if security is told in advance. Give your coordinator the building address and a contact at the site, and we will arrange a window during business hours.
Will a carrier come up into the Los Gatos or Saratoga hills?
Full-size haulers cannot safely navigate narrow canyon roads, so pickups above the Village or in the Saratoga hills are arranged at a meeting point on a main road nearby. Your coordinator will confirm the spot before the truck is dispatched.
How far in advance should I book a San Jose car shipment?
Seven to ten days ahead is ideal, especially from May through August when relocations peak. We can often assign a carrier within one to three days for common lanes, but more notice means a wider choice of trucks and a better rate.
